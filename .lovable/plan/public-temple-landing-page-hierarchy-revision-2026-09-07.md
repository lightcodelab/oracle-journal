# Public Temple landing-page hierarchy revision

## Goal
Rework the existing public root page into the requested conversion sequence without changing the established Temple palette, typography, legal wording, authentication routes, membership prices, offer dates, or checkout rules.

## What will change

1. **Replace only the hero photograph**
   - Upload the approved `landing-page-banner.png` through the project asset system.
   - Rebuild the hero as a 16:9 landscape threshold with live HTML copy over the image's dark left side; no text will be baked into or alter the photograph.
   - Keep the woman and doorway visible on small screens with `object-position: 65% center` and maintain an accessible descriptive alt.
   - Keep the supplied eyebrow, headline, body, primary CTA, microcopy, and quiet Sign In access.

2. **Make the pre-opening CTA actionable**
   - Before 14 September 2026, **Enter The Temple** will lead into the existing founding sign-up/invitation route and retain the pending membership intent.
   - Once the backend offer reports checkout as available, the same CTA will use the existing checkout flow.
   - Preserve current analytics events and checkout-completion tracking.

3. **Apply the exact page hierarchy**
   - Hero.
   - Recognition.
   - Core promise.
   - A separate five-part editorial process: Notice, Meet, Choose, Record, Return.
   - Five-door overview in the requested order: AreekeerA® Guide, Living Pattern Lab, Remembrance, Devotion, Communion.
   - Why this is different / return.
   - Founder credibility and the AreekeerA® Method, using the real Julie & Tash photograph and established factual copy.
   - Real testimonials only; the existing admin-only empty state remains until approved testimonials exist.
   - Founding Membership invitation and primary CTA, preserving live pricing and cancellation language.
   - A clearly separate, lower-priority Physical Temple section, restoring the established physical-offering details without presenting them as membership inclusions.
   - FAQ, followed by the final threshold CTA.

4. **Remove hierarchy conflicts**
   - Remove the current Who It Is For / Who It Is Not For block from this ordered version.
   - Keep the former “One membership. The whole Temple.” grid out of the early page; its useful product information remains expressed through the five editorial door panels.
   - Avoid icon grids and generic card styling; use unframed editorial layouts, restrained rules, and generous spacing. Pricing remains the deliberate framed element.
